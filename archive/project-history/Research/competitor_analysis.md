# Competitor Analysis: Modest Fashion (Evidence-Based)

## 1. Modanisa
- **Mobile UX (7/10):** Robust but overwhelming. Search is the primary driver.
- **Trust (8/10):** Strong due to massive scale and explicit security messaging.
- **Worth Copying:** The level of granular filtering (Material, Length, Pattern).
- **Avoid:** The clutter in the mobile header which can distract from the products.

## 2. Haute Hijab
- **Mobile UX (9/10):** Exceptional PDP layout. Education-first approach.
- **Trust (9/10):** Built through community (UGC) and deep expertise (Fabric guides).
- **Worth Copying:** The "Fabric Guide" (Smooth vs Textured, Light vs Heavy) and community review photo gallery.
- **Avoid:** High price anchoring if the target for فضفاض is "affordable."

## 3. EMMA (Egypt)
- **Mobile UX (7/10):** Beautiful but has friction points (forced login for checkout).
- **Trust (8/10):** Local market leader for premium hijabs. High community engagement.
- **Worth Copying:** The "Verdict" section in reviews and the focus on "Community."
- **Avoid:** Forced login before checkout; it kills conversion for new customers.

## 4. Saqhoute (Egypt)
- **Mobile UX (8/10):** Clean Shopify experience.
- **Trust (9/10):** Built through artisanal stories and "Slow Fashion" ethics.
- **Worth Copying:** Clear labeling of "100% Egyptian Cotton" and local payment installments (Sympl).
- **Avoid:** 404 links or broken product paths (observed during live audit).
