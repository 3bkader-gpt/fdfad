## Strategic Takeaways for "فضفاض"

### Features to Copy

- **Haute Hijab:** Editorial-style photography and styling advice to build authority.
- **Modanisa:** Intuitive, category-driven navigation and robust filtering (crucial for high-inventory ecommerce).
- **AabCollection:** Clean, minimal product page layouts that focus on fabric quality and drape.

### Features to Avoid

- Aggressive promotional pop-ups that degrade mobile usability (Modanisa).
- Cluttered, busy mobile menus (Sefamerve).
- Generic, low-effort product cards that don't clearly display material information.
