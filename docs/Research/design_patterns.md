# Observed Design Patterns for "فضفاض"

Based on the live browser audit of 7 major brands, the following patterns are recommended for implementation.

## 1. The Fabric Sensory Pattern
- **Observation:** Both Haute Hijab and EMMA use specific icons or scales to describe fabric (Opacity, Weight, Texture).
- **Rationale:** In modest fashion, "touch" is missing. Digital sensory cues reduce return rates and sizing anxiety.
- **Application for فضفاض:** Implement an "Opacity Meter" (1-5) and "Fabric Weight" pill on every PDP.

## 2. The Community Proof Loop (UGC)
- **Observation:** Haute Hijab's community gallery and EMMA's "636k EMMAs" footer links.
- **Rationale:** High trust is built by showing real women wearing the product.
- **Application for فضفاض:** Dedicate a section of the PDP to "Styled by You" using Instagram mentions.

## 3. The Local Trust Stack (Egypt)
- **Observation:** Saqhoute and EMMA emphasize "Made in Egypt" and local payment methods (Sympl, ValU, COD).
- **Rationale:** Egyptian customers value local craftsmanship and need flexible payment for higher-priced sets.
- **Application for فضفاض:** Highlight "Made in Cairo" and "Cash on Delivery" within 50px of the "Add to Cart" button.

## 4. The Sticky Mobile Converter
- **Observation:** Most high-converting Shopify stores (Veiled, Aab) use a sticky bottom bar for mobile checkout.
- **Rationale:** Eliminates scrolling friction.
- **Application for فضفاض:** Add to cart and Checkout should always be reachable with the thumb in the bottom 20% of the screen.

## 5. The Content-as-Commerce Pattern
- **Observation:** Haute Hijab includes "How to Wear it" videos directly on the product page.
- **Rationale:** Increases time-on-site and builds authority as a modest fashion expert.
- **Application for فضفاض:** Short, 10-second looping "Drape Videos" instead of static 2nd/3rd images.
