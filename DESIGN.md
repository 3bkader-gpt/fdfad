# Design Context: FADFAAD

- **Color Strategy:** Restrained (Brand hue: primary `#2C3E35`, accent `#C89B7E`).
- **Typography:** Brand-serif (font-serif) for headings, geist-sans (font-sans) for UI/text.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA required for all status indicators, labels, and text-input states.
- **Component Aesthetic:**
  - Cards: subtle borders, not heavy shadow.
  - Pills: high contrast semantic colors, clearly distinguished from neutrals.
  - Buttons: rounded, luxury feel, bold typography.
