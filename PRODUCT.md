# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary users are shoppers in Egypt looking for high-quality modest fashion (Abayas, Jilbabs, etc.) and admins managing the collection. Shoppers seek a high-trust, luxury boutique experience that overcomes the friction of manual WhatsApp ordering.

## Product Purpose
Fadfaad is a mobile-first modest fashion platform that streamlines the ordering process. It transforms the "WhatsApp-only" workflow into a structured digital journey, ensuring professional inventory management and a premium customer experience.

## Brand Personality
Serene, Elegant, Trustworthy. The voice is calm and sophisticated, reflecting the art of modest drapery. Emotional goals include confidence in quality/modesty and delight in a seamless journey.

## Anti-references
- Generic cluttered dropshipping templates (AliExpress).
- Aggressive sales pop-ups or "urgency" hacks.
- Dense multi-column mobile grids (prefer clean 2-column portrait).
- Heavy gradients or overly decorative glassmorphism.
- Native browser alerts (prefer stylized notifications).

## Design Principles
1. **The Art of Drapery**: Visuals must emphasize fabric quality, flow, and opacity.
2. **MENA Trust Mastery**: Checkout and contact points must feel secure and culturally aligned.
3. **Structured Simplicity**: Replace manual friction with intuitive digital affordances (e.g., smart forms, real-time sync).
4. **Mobile-First Luxury**: Spacing and hierarchy optimized for portrait screens and focused interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG Level**: AA compliance required.
- **Visuals**: High contrast for status indicators and input states.
- **Inclusion**: RTL support as the primary direction; focus on inclusive sizing and clear modesty metrics (opacity scale).
